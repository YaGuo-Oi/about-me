About Me!

My name is Ya Guo, and I will become a user experience designer. In the early days of my design awareness, I was addicted to the use of electronic software for a long time. As I used it, I became interested in the interface layout, which made me realize maybe I can also be part of those interface designers.

The reason that supports me to persist in design is that I will get great satisfaction when good design changes the life experience of more people.

In this job, I really enjoy creating useful, usable, credible, accessible and valuable designs for different groups of people and based on different needs.
